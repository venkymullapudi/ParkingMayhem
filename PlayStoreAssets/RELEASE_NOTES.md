# Parking Rush — Release Notes

## What's New (this update)

🚗 **Real vehicles for every world** — park cars, trucks and buses on the Road;
boats, yachts and ships on the Water; planes, jets and helicopters in the Air;
rockets, shuttles and satellites in Space. New vehicle types unlock as you climb levels.

🌍 **Four playable terrains** — choose Road, Water, Air or Space when you hit Play.
Each one handles differently: waves push you around, wind gusts shove you off course,
and zero-G space makes everything drift.

🅿️ **Smarter parking** — the parking spot now shows the exact silhouette of your
vehicle, so you know precisely how to fit in.

🏙️ **Busy lots** — from level 7, navigate around other parked vehicles.

🎯 **A real difficulty curve** — gentle, puzzle-like early levels that ramp up:
angled spots, moving spots, tighter tolerances, a true turning radius at high
levels, and bizarre shapes once you're a true parking master.

✨ **Fresh look** — themed backgrounds per world, glossy vehicles, a redesigned
main menu, working settings (music/SFX/vibration) and an achievements page.

**Full description:**
Parking Rush is a relaxing-to-frantic precision parking puzzler across four
worlds — Road, Water, Air and Space. Slide your vehicle into the glowing spot,
hold it steady, and move on. Sounds easy? Wind, waves, zero-gravity drift, moving
spots and busy lots are waiting. Start with a simple car in an empty lot and work
your way up to docking a satellite in deep space. Hundreds of levels, a gentle
learning curve, and a challenge that never stops climbing.

- 4 distinct worlds, each with its own physics
- Dozens of real vehicle types
- Silhouette-matched parking spots
- Gradually escalating challenge
- Achievements to chase
